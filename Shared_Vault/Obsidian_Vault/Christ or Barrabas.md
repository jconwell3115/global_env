## 🕊️ **Message: Is MAGA “Choosing Barabbas Over Christ”**

TLDR version:  A movement whose leader rides on a warhorse does not follow a movement whose leader rides on the colt of a donkey. MAGA is a pseudo-Christian oppresive authoritarian movement that Christians shouldn't support. 


**Palm Sunday.** Crowds lined the streets of Jerusalem, waving palm branches and shouting _“Hosanna!”_—a cry that means _“Save us!”_ They believed Jesus had come to overthrow Roman oppression, reestablish the throne of David and restore Israel’s greatness. They wanted a political savior, a warrior king, someone to make Israel great again.

But Jesus came riding on the colt of a donkey, not a warhorse. A symbol of peace, not conquest. His kingdom was not of this world. He came not to crush Rome, but to **crush sin**. Not to raise up Israel’s power, but to raise up the humble, the poor, the broken.

The palm branches were a symbol of **national pride and victory**—used in Jewish tradition to celebrate military triumph. But the people misunderstood. Their cries of _Hosanna_ were sincere, but misdirected. They wanted salvation on their terms, not God’s.

And when Jesus didn’t meet their expectations, they turned on Him.

Just days later, the same crowd was given a choice: **Jesus or Barabbas**.

Barabbas was a violent insurrectionist. A man who had taken up arms against Rome. A nationalist. A fighter. He represented the kind of power the people wanted and his methods to his desired goal aligned with theirs. 

Instead, Jesus represented **sacrificial love, humility, and truth**.

And they chose Barabbas.

---

## 🔥 **The Modern Parallel**

Today, many Christians face a similar choice.

- Do we choose the way of **Christ**—the way of peace, humility, truth, and sacrificial love?
- Or do we choose the way of **Barabbas**—the way of power, violence, nationalism, and domination?


The MAGA movement often promotes a vision of America where only certain people are truly welcome—those who look, think, speak, and believe a certain way.
Its emphasis on political conquest, exclusion of others, and contempt for enemies, mirrors the crowd’s desire for a messiah who would “make Israel great again.” It promises salvation through physical strength and self-righteous works, not surrender. Through domination, not servant leadership.

But Jesus mourned over Jerusalem, saying:

> _“If you, even you, had only known on this day what would bring you peace. But now they are hidden from your eyes.”_ (Luke 19:42)

He wept because they didn’t understand. They missed the heart of His mission.

---

## 🙏 **A Call to Reflection**

Let us not repeat the mistake of this crowd. Let us not wave palm branches while misunderstanding the cross. Let us not cry _Hosanna_ while rejecting the Savior’s true purpose.

Jesus didn’t come to build an empire of forced capitulation and oppression. He came to build a kingdom of **grace, justice, and mercy**.

The question remains:  
**Will we choose Christ—or Barabbas?**

---

### 🔥 **“Conditional Patriotism and the Gospel of Power”**

The MAGA movement often promotes a vision of America where only certain people are truly welcome—those who look, think, speak, and believe a certain way. If you don’t fit the mold—whether because of race, religion, immigration status, gender identity, or political views—you’re treated not just as different, but as **dangerous**, **un-American**, or even **evil**.

This is **not the way of Christ**.

- Jesus welcomed the outsider: the Samaritan woman, the Roman centurion, the tax collector, the leper.
- He broke social and religious barriers to show that **God’s love is not only reserved for the “acceptable.”**
- He warned against the kind of **self-righteous gatekeeping** that MAGA often embodies:  
  > *“Woe to you… you shut the door of the kingdom of heaven in people’s faces.”* (Matthew 23:13)

---

### 🧠 **Key Themes to Describe MAGA’s Treatment of Non-Conformers**

- **Tribalism over compassion**: Loyalty to the group is valued more than empathy for the outsider.
- **Purity tests**: People are judged by political alignment, not character or conscience.
- **Contempt for the weak**: Those who are vulnerable or different are mocked, not protected.
- **Fear-based identity**: The movement thrives on the idea that “others” are threats to be defeated, not neighbors to be loved.
