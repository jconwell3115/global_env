**Devotional: The Gift That Gives Nothing Back**

Many today speak about strength, self-reliance, and national pride as marks of faithfulness. Yet Jesus calls us to something radically different. In Luke 6:35, He commands us to love, do good, and lend with no expectation of return. In Luke 14:14, He reminds us that our reward comes not from those who can repay but from God Himself.

This teaching stands in sharp contrast to modern movements that exalt the prosperous while dismissing the poor. The Kingdom of God does not run on reciprocity or political calculation. It runs on compassion that looks like foolishness to the proud.

What the poor give us is something far greater than material return. They offer us a sacred opportunity to share God's love in its purest form—without condition, without transaction, without applause. That moment of giving, born of grace and not gain, is a gift in itself. It is there that we taste the joy of the Kingdom, where generosity becomes worship and the act of love is its own reward.

When policies or attitudes prioritize “us first” and leave the vulnerable behind, they simply do not reflect the heart of Christ. To follow Jesus is to live as if mercy, not might, defines what truly makes a nation great.

**Prayer:**  
Lord Jesus, soften our hearts where we have grown hard or proud. Teach us to see the poor and forgotten as You see them—beloved and worthy of care. Help us recognize that every act of compassion is a chance to share Your love. Free us from fear of loss, and make us generous as You are generous. Let our giving reveal the beauty of Your kingdom and the joy of serving others. Amen.
