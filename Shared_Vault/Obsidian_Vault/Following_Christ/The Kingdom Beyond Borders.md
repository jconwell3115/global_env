
**Devotional: The Kingdom Beyond Borders**  

“Seek ye first the kingdom of God and His righteousness, and all these things shall be added unto you.” — Matthew 6:33  

When Jesus spoke these words, He wasn’t calling us to build a political order but to pursue a kingdom not of this world. The kingdom of God transcends national borders, languages, and movements—it gathers every tribe and tongue under one King who reigns in justice and mercy.  

Yet, many today narrow their allegiance to earthly causes, mistaking temporal power for eternal purpose. In doing so, they risk turning nations—and even movements—into idols. The cry to “make a nation great” can subtly shift our devotion from the King of Heaven to the kingdoms of men, from worship to nationalism, from trust in God to pride in self.  

Earthly kingdoms, no matter how noble in intent, are fleeting. When we elevate them beyond their place, we forge golden calves out of flags and slogans, forgetting that no earthly throne can share space with the throne of Christ.  

God’s kingdom is not built on political strength or cultural dominance; it is built on love, humility, and the redemption of the people's for whom Christ died. When we truly seek first God’s kingdom, our loyalties are purified. We learn to honor our nations without idolizing them, to love our people without excluding others, and to lift our eyes from the temporal to the eternal.  

Let us seek first His kingdom, not a smaller one of our own making. Let our loyalty be to the eternal King who unites, not the earthly agendas and idols that divide. When our hearts are set on Him, everything else finds its rightful place beneath His rule.
