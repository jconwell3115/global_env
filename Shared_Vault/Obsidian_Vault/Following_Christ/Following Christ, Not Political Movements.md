# Following Christ, Not Political Movements

## Devotional: Following Christ, Not Political Movements

### Scripture Reading:
Matthew 25:35 – "For I was hungry and you gave me food, I was thirsty and you gave me drink, I was a stranger and you welcomed me." Blue Letter Bible (ESV)


The teachings of Jesus emphasize compassion, hospitality, and justice toward those in need, especially the stranger and immigrant. Jesus calls us to see the vulnerable as if they were Him, underscoring that welcoming the stranger is a hallmark of true discipleship. The biblical command to "love your neighbor as yourself" includes those who are outsiders or foreigners (Leviticus 19:33-34).In contrast, many political movements like MAGA have policies and rhetoric that prioritize exclusion, strict enforcement, and suspicion of immigrants. These positions diverge from Christ’s call to mercy, humility, and love. While political agendas weigh concerns of security and sovereignty, Christians are challenged to hold fast to the gospel’s ethic of unconditional compassion.To truly follow Christ means embodying His love for strangers, welcoming the marginalized, and practicing justice as a reflection of God's kingdom. This calls believers to stand apart when political allegiances conflict with the gospel message and to lovingly point out where actions diverge from Christ’s teachings.

#### Prayer:
Lord Jesus, open our eyes to see the stranger as You see them. Fill our hearts with compassion and mercy, that we might welcome and care for those in need, reflecting Your love. Help us to live out Your teaching boldly and lovingly, especially when it challenges the prevailing views of our society. May our actions and words bring glory to Your name alone. Amen.
