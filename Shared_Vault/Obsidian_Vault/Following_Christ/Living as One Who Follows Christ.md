# Living as One Who Follows Christ

#### **Scripture Reading:**
- Romans 12:1–2 
***

### Living as One Who Follows Christ

To live as a Christian is more than a confession; it is a daily surrender of the heart and a transformation of the mind. Paul urges in Romans 12:1–2 that we present our bodies as living sacrifices, holy and pleasing to God. This is the foundation of true worship: giving our entire selves to God and allowing Him to renew our minds so that our thoughts and desires align with His will.

Yet, this surrender meets resistance. In Romans 7:18, Paul confesses that though he desires to do what is good, he lacks the strength to carry it out. The struggle every believer faces—the pull of the flesh against the Spirit—reveals our utter dependence on divine help. The mind set on the flesh is hostile to God (Romans 8:7), incapable of submitting to His law. Apart from the Spirit, even our best intentions fall short.

But through Christ, we have died to that old nature so that we may belong to Him who was raised from the dead, bearing fruit for God (Romans 7:4). The key is not striving harder in our own strength but being led by the Spirit who dwells within. As 1 Corinthians 12:3 reminds us, no one can truly confess that Jesus is Lord except by the Holy Spirit. Our obedience flows not from external compulsion but from inward transformation—God working in us both to will and to act for His good pleasure (Philippians 2:13).

Love then becomes the measure of this new life. “For this is love for God: that we keep His commandments” (1 John 5:3). Obedience is not burdensome because it springs from a renewed heart—a life in which the Spirit enables what once felt impossible. When we walk in this truth, even our struggles point us to grace. In our weakness, we lean more fully on His strength. In our doubts, we remember we are called not to self-reliance but to faithful surrender.

The Spirit Himself bears witness that we are children of God, leading us in hope, sustaining us through suffering, and shaping us into the likeness of Christ until the day our redemption is complete (Romans 8:24–25). To live as one who follows Christ, then, is to continually yield—to let the life of Jesus find its full expression in us.

***

### Closing Prayer

Lord, help me to offer myself as a living sacrifice, surrendered fully to Your will. Renew my mind and transform my heart so that I may follow You faithfully each day. Strengthen me through Your Spirit to overcome my weaknesses and live in obedience that flows from love. May Your life shine through me, drawing others to You. In Jesus' name, Amen.

***

### Reflection Question

In what area of your life do you need to surrender more fully to God’s transforming work, trusting Him to empower your obedience and renew your mind?
