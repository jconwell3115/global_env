# Love Beyond Partisan Loyalty

#### **Scripture Reading:**
- John 15:12 — “This is my commandment, that you love one another as I have loved you.”

Jesus didn’t offer a suggestion—He gave a command: love one another as He loved us. His love is self-giving, sacrificial, and unconditional, crossing boundaries of tribe, politics, and nation. It is love without exception or favoritism.

This verse challenges a culture in which power and identity politics often eclipse compassion. The “Make America Great Again” ideology appeals to loyalty, control, and exclusion—values that stand in opposition to Christ’s law of love. When policies demean immigrants, disregard the poor, or excuse cruelty in the name of “national security” or “strength,” they reveal a love that measures worth by allegiance, not by inherent human dignity.

Christ calls believers to something radically different: to love without condition, even those who oppose us. That command dismantles any movement—left or right—that depends on division or resentment to thrive. If our politics require us to despise others, then we’ve stepped outside the command of Christ. The cross does not wave a flag; it calls us to die to self and live for one another.

Prayer:
Lord Jesus, teach us to love as You love—without borders, fear, or pride. Keep us from confusing political zeal with godly compassion. Give us courage to stand for truth, justice, and mercy even when our culture rewards hate. May Your command to love be our highest loyalty. Amen.

Further Study:1 John 4:7–8 — God is love, and whoever loves is born of God.

Romans 12:9–21 — Overcome evil with good.

Galatians 5:14 — The whole law is fulfilled in one word: “You shall love your neighbor as yourself.”
