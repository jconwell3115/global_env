Here is a structured fasting program that combines regular intermittent 16:8 fasting with a monthly 3-day water fast followed by a 2-day gradual refeeding plan:

***

### Weekly Routine (Intermittent 16:8 Fasting)
- **Fasting window:** 16 hours (e.g., 8 PM to 12 PM the next day)
- **Eating window:** 8 hours (12 PM to 8 PM)
- **Daily habits:**
  - Eat balanced, nutrient-dense meals within the 8-hour window.
  - Focus on whole foods with protein, healthy fats, fiber, and moderate carbs.
  - Stay hydrated with water, herbal tea, or black coffee during fasting.
  - Incorporate exercise (a mix of aerobic and strength training) on most days.

***

### Monthly Extended Fast Cycle

#### Day 1-3: Water Fast
- Only consume water, herbal teas (no sweeteners), and electrolytes if needed.
- Rest more than usual, avoid high-intensity physical activity.
- Stay hydrated and listen to your body.
- Monitor for any adverse symptoms; discontinue if unwell and seek advice.

#### Day 4-5: Refeeding Phase
- **Day 4:** Break fast gently with small portions of broth, diluted vegetable juices, and light cooked vegetables like zucchini or spinach.
- **Day 5:** Slowly add soft foods such as steamed vegetables, eggs, avocado, and lean protein like chicken or fish.
- Avoid heavy carbs, processed foods, and sugars.
- Continue small, spaced meals and hydrate well.
- Gradually return to your 16:8 routine following completion of Day 5.

***

### Notes & Safety Tips
- This program is designed for generally healthy adults. Consult a healthcare provider before starting, especially with chronic health conditions or medications.
- Adjust fasting and refeeding pace based on personal tolerance.
- Prioritize sleep, stress management, and nutrient quality throughout the program.
- Avoid overeating during eating windows and refeed days.

***

This combined approach leverages daily intermittent fasting benefits and periodic extended fasting’s metabolic benefits while minimizing risks and supporting digestive health via a careful refeeding plan.
